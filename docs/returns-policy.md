## 1. Our commitment

Shoppie aims to deliver the products customers ordered in good condition. This policy explains how to report a damaged, spoiled, incorrect, missing, or otherwise defective item and how Shoppie may resolve the issue. It applies to fictional Shoppie orders in India. Nothing in this policy removes a right available under applicable consumer or other law.

## 2. Report a problem

Contact Shoppie at **support@shoppie.example** or **+91 80000 12345**. Include:

- The order number and the affected product(s).
- A brief description of the issue and when it was noticed.
- Clear photos of the product, label, and packaging when available. Do not delay reporting a safety issue to take photos.
- For a missing item, a photo of the delivered items and invoice, if available.

Sample reporting windows:

- **Perishable goods:** report damage, spoilage, incorrect items, or temperature concerns within **24 hours of delivery**, and before the labeled use-by date where relevant.
- **Non-perishable goods:** report damage, missing or incorrect items, or a suspected defect within **7 calendar days of delivery**.
- **Food safety or allergy concern:** stop consuming the product and contact Shoppie as soon as possible, even if the sample reporting window has passed.

These windows help Shoppie investigate promptly. They do not limit any rights available under applicable law.

## 3. Perishable products

For fresh produce, dairy, chilled or frozen goods, prepared foods, and other perishable products:

- Returns for preference-based reasons (such as taste, change of mind, or ordering the wrong quantity) are generally not accepted after delivery because of food safety and resale concerns.
- If a product is spoiled, damaged, leaking, unsafe, incorrect, or missing, Shoppie will review the report. Depending on the circumstances, Shoppie may provide a replacement, refund, or account credit.
- Keep the affected item and its packaging when safe to do so until Shoppie responds. Do not consume or return a product that appears unsafe. Shoppie may ask for photos or arrange a pickup where appropriate.
- If an item is recalled or presents a potential safety risk, Shoppie may ask the customer to stop using it and follow specific return, disposal, or refund instructions.

## 4. Non-perishable products

For shelf-stable or durable goods:

- Products must generally be unused, unwashed, unassembled where applicable, and returned with original packaging, tags, accessories, and proof of purchase.
- A change-of-mind return may be requested within **7 calendar days of delivery** for products marked **Returnable** on their product page. Some sealed hygiene, personal-care, food, customized, or clearance products may be marked non-returnable, subject to applicable law.
- Products that arrive damaged, defective, incomplete, or different from the order may be eligible for a replacement or refund even when marked non-returnable, subject to review and applicable law.
- Shoppie may inspect returned products before approving a replacement or refund. A return request can be declined if the returned item does not match the reported issue or is damaged through use, unless applicable law provides otherwise.

## 5. How to request a return

1. Contact support within the applicable sample reporting window, providing the order number and reason.
2. Shoppie will respond within **2 business days** with an approval, request for more information, or explanation.
3. If approved, Shoppie will provide pickup or return instructions. Do not ship an item to Shoppie without those instructions.
4. Pack the item securely with its included parts. For a food safety issue, follow support's instructions and do not send an unsafe item through ordinary post.
5. After the item is received or the issue is verified, Shoppie will confirm the resolution.

Sample pickup is free for an item that arrived damaged, defective, incorrect, or incomplete. For an eligible change-of-mind return, the customer may be responsible for a **₹75** return pickup fee, disclosed here as synthetic sample data, where permitted by applicable law. Any fee that applies will be confirmed before the return is arranged.

## 6. Available resolutions

Depending on the product, reported issue, stock, and customer preference, Shoppie may offer:

- A replacement of the same item, if available.
- A refund to the original payment method.
- A Shoppie account credit, if the customer agrees.
- A partial refund when only part of an order is affected and that is an appropriate resolution.

Shoppie will not substitute account credit for a refund where a refund is required by applicable law. If a replacement is unavailable, Shoppie will offer an appropriate refund or other remedy.

## 7. Refunds

- Once a refund is approved, Shoppie will initiate it within **2 business days**.
- Refunds are normally sent to the original payment method. The sample time for the credit to appear is **5–10 business days**, depending on the bank or payment provider.
- Delivery fees are refunded when the entire order is cancelled or the delivery issue is attributable to Shoppie, unless the fee relates to unaffected items or applicable law requires another treatment.
- Any account credit is issued only with the customer's agreement and will have a sample validity of **12 months** from issue, subject to applicable law and the displayed credit terms.
- If the sample refund window passes without the credit appearing, contact support with the order number and refund reference.

## 8. Exchanges

Shoppie does not offer direct size or preference exchanges in this sample policy. Customers may request a return for an eligible non-perishable item and place a separate order for the desired item. A replacement for a damaged, defective, incorrect, or incomplete product is handled as a service resolution and does not require a new purchase.
