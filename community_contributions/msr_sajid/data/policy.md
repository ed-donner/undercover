# Wayfarer Mini Policy (fictional, written for this demo)

## 1. Baggage and personal effects
Accidental damage or theft of belongings during the trip is covered.
- Per-item limit: $300. The claim is paid on the lower of the amount claimed and $300.
- Deductible: $50 per claim, taken off the payout.
- Theft needs a police report. If the claim has no police report, refer it to a colleague.
- Damage needs a repair report or a statement that the item cannot be repaired.

## 2. Flight delay
- $50 for each full 4 hours of delay, up to $200 in total.
- Needs a delay letter from the airline.

## 3. Trip cancellation
Covered only when the traveler or a close family member falls ill, is injured or dies,
and a doctor's note is attached. Changing your mind or a better offer is not covered: decline.

## 4. Emergency medical
Medical bills during the trip are paid in full up to $5,000. No deductible. Receipts are required.

## 5. Decisions
- approve: covered and the required documents are attached. Work out the payout.
- decline: the policy does not cover it.
- refer: a required document is missing or the story and documents disagree.
