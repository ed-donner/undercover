# UnderCover Travel Insurance: Policy Wording, short version 2

All amounts are in US dollars. The policy record shows the plan, the add-ons, the dates of the covered trip, the trip cost and the travelers.

## 1. Your plan

| Benefit | Economy | Premium | First |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trip Cancellation and Interruption, maximum | 1,500 | 5,000 | 10,000 |
| Trip Delay, waiting period, hours | 12 | 6 | 3 |
| Trip Delay, maximum | 300 | 800 | 1,500 |
| Baggage Delay, waiting period, hours | 24 | 12 | 6 |
| Baggage Delay, maximum | 100 | 300 | 500 |
| Baggage and Personal Effects, maximum | 750 | 1,500 | 3,000 |
| Per-item limit | 150 | 300 | 500 |
| Passport and Travel Documents, maximum | 150 | 250 | 400 |
| Emergency Medical, maximum | 25,000 | 100,000 | 250,000 |
| Deductible | 100 | 50 | 0 |

The deductible applies once per claim to Baggage and Personal Effects and to Emergency Medical, and to no other benefit.

## 2. Trip Cancellation and Interruption

Covered reasons only: sickness, injury or death of the traveler, a traveling companion or a family member, confirmed in writing by a physician; severe weather or a natural disaster that stops the carrier's service for 24 hours or more; home or destination made uninhabitable by fire, flood or storm; jury duty or a court subpoena; losing a job through no fault of their own after at least one year with the employer.

Cancellation pays the prepaid trip costs that suppliers will not refund. Interruption pays the unused prepaid costs that will not be refunded, plus a one-way economy fare home. Pay up to the trip cost on the policy record or the plan maximum, whichever is lower. Refunds and credits from suppliers are taken off first.

## 3. Trip Delay

The carrier delays the traveler for at least the waiting period because of a mechanical or crew problem, a canceled service, severe weather or a strike. The delay runs from the scheduled departure time to the time the traveler actually departs. Pays reasonable extra costs for meals, accommodation, local transport and rebooking fees during the delay, up to the maximum. Needs the receipts and the carrier's confirmation of the delay and its cause. If a delay on the way home keeps the traveler traveling past the return date, cover continues until they get home.

## 4. Baggage Delay

The carrier delivers the checked baggage at least the waiting period after the traveler arrives at the destination. Pays essential clothing and toiletries bought while waiting, up to the maximum. Needs the receipts and the carrier's delayed baggage report. Not on the journey home.

## 5. Baggage and Personal Effects

Loss, theft or accidental damage to baggage and personal belongings during the covered trip, up to the maximum.

- The per-item limit applies to every item. A pair or a set, such as a pair of shoes, a suit or a camera with its lens, counts as one item.
- The carrier pays first. Anything a carrier, hotel or other party pays for the same loss is taken off.
- Theft must be reported to the local police within 24 hours, and needs a copy of the police report.
- Loss or damage by a carrier must be reported to the carrier, and needs a copy of its report.
- Accidental damage needs a repair estimate or a statement that the item cannot be repaired.

Not covered:

- Belongings left unattended. Unattended means out of sight and out of reach in a place the public can get to. Belongings in a vehicle are unattended unless they are locked in the trunk or the glove compartment and out of sight.
- Cash above $100.
- Wear and tear, or gradual deterioration.
- Damage caused by moths, vermin, insects or pets.
- The value of information. For a laptop, a drive, a notebook or a document, pay for the physical item, not the data, the writing or the work it holds.

## 6. Passport and Travel Documents

A passport or visa lost, stolen, damaged or destroyed during the covered trip: the replacement fees, the photos and reasonable local travel to the embassy or consulate, up to the maximum. Needs the receipts and a short account of what happened.

## 7. Emergency Medical

Necessary treatment of a sudden sickness or injury during the covered trip, up to the maximum. Needs the itemized bill and the medical records from the visit.

Not covered:

- Pre-existing conditions. A condition is pre-existing if, in the 60 days before the policy was bought, the traveler had symptoms of it, saw a physician about it, received treatment for it or had a change in medication for it. The Pre-Existing Condition Waiver removes this exclusion.
- Injuries from adventure sports, unless the policy has the Adventure Sports add-on.
- Treatment that can reasonably wait until the traveler is home.

## 8. Add-ons

**Adventure Sports.** Adventure sports are bungee jumping, skydiving, paragliding, scuba diving deeper than 10 meters (33 feet), heli-skiing, roped mountaineering and white-water rafting above grade 3. With this add-on, injuries from these sports are covered, provided the traveler holds any certification the activity requires and stays within its limits.

**Pre-Existing Condition Waiver.** With this add-on the pre-existing condition exclusion in section 7 does not apply.

**Electronics Plus.** With this add-on the per-item limit for electronics is $1,200. Electronics are phones, tablets, laptops, cameras and their lenses, e-readers and smartwatches.

## 9. General exclusions

No benefit pays for: anything that happens outside the dates of the covered trip; events that were publicly known before the policy was bought, such as a named storm or an announced strike; consequential losses, which follow indirectly from an incident, such as lost earnings, missed events or contractual penalties; losses while breaking the law or impaired by alcohol or drugs; war, civil unrest and nuclear incidents; any claim supported by false statements or altered documents.

## 10. How we work out the payment

1. Start with the eligible amount of each item or expense. For belongings that is the amount claimed or the per-item limit, whichever is lower.
2. Take off anything a carrier or another party has paid for the same loss.
3. Take off the deductible, where it applies.
4. Pay the result, up to the maximum for the benefit.

## 11. How to claim

File within 90 days of the incident, with the documents named in the section for the benefit. If a required document is missing, refer the claim.

## 12. Definitions

- **Carrier:** an airline, railway, ferry, cruise or coach company that the traveler has a ticket with.
- **Covered trip:** the trip on the policy record, from the departure date to the return date.
- **Family member:** a spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent or grandchild.
- **Physician:** a licensed medical doctor who is not the traveler, a traveling companion or a family member.
- **Traveling companion:** a person named as a traveler on the policy record.
