# Shoppie Cancellation Policy

> **Synthetic sample policy:** This document is fictional starter content for product development and must be reviewed and adapted before being used with customers.

**Effective date:** 1 November 2026  
**Last updated:** 1 November 2026

## 1. Scope

This policy explains when a Shoppie customer may cancel an order and how refunds are handled. It applies to orders placed through the fictional Shoppie website or mobile app in India. A customer's statutory rights remain unaffected.

## 2. Customer-requested cancellations

- A customer may cancel an order at no charge while its status is **Placed** or **Confirmed**, provided packing or preparation has not started.
- Once an order is **Being packed**, **Prepared**, or **Dispatched**, the cancellation option may no longer be available. Contact support as soon as possible; Shoppie will check whether fulfillment can still be stopped.
- Perishable orders may enter preparation soon after confirmation. Shoppie may be unable to cancel them once preparation, picking, or temperature-controlled packing begins.
- To request cancellation, use **Orders → Cancel order** in the account or contact the sample support team at **support@shoppie.example** with the order number. The fictional sample support phone is **+91 80000 12345**.
- If cancellation is accepted before dispatch, the full amount paid for the cancelled items, including delivery fees charged solely for that order, will be refunded. If only some items are cancelled, the order total and any fee threshold may be recalculated and shown to the customer.

## 3. Failed delivery and refused orders

- Shoppie or its delivery partner may make up to **two delivery attempts** when the recipient is unavailable, subject to route and product suitability.
- If delivery cannot be completed because the address is incorrect, access is unavailable, or the customer cannot be reached, Shoppie may cancel the order. For non-perishable goods, a reasonable return-to-origin or handling fee of up to **₹75** may be deducted only if it was disclosed before checkout and permitted by applicable law.
- For perishable goods, a failed delivery may make the products unsafe to resell. Shoppie may not be able to refund those goods when the failure is attributable to the customer, subject to applicable law and a case review. Shoppie will refund any undelivered items when the failure is attributable to Shoppie or its delivery partner.
- If a customer refuses delivery because the package is visibly damaged, leaking, incorrect, or appears unsafe, the customer should ask the delivery partner to record the reason and contact support. The case will be handled under the Returns Policy, without an avoidable cancellation penalty.

## 4. Cancellations by Shoppie

Shoppie may cancel all or part of an order if a product is unavailable, a listing or price contains a material error, payment is not confirmed, the address is outside the service area, delivery cannot reasonably be completed, or fraud or misuse is reasonably suspected. Shoppie will notify the customer using the contact information provided with the order. Shoppie will refund the amount paid for any cancelled items to the original payment method.

## 5. Refund timing and method

- Approved cancellation refunds are initiated within **2 business days** after cancellation is confirmed.
- The refund is sent to the original payment method. The sample estimate for the amount to appear is **5–10 business days**, depending on the bank, card issuer, wallet, or payment provider.
- Cash-on-delivery orders that were paid at the door are not charged if cancelled before handover. If a cash-on-delivery payment was collected and a refund is due, support will arrange a bank transfer or another mutually agreed method after verifying the customer.
- Shoppie cannot control the time taken by a bank or payment provider after it initiates a refund. Customers may contact support with the order number if the sample window passes.

## 6. Promotions and partial cancellations

When an order using a promotion is partially cancelled, the promotion may be recalculated based on the remaining eligible items and the offer terms shown at checkout. Any resulting change to the refund will be explained to the customer. Shoppie will not charge a cancellation fee that was not disclosed before the order was placed.

## 7. Contact

For cancellation help, contact **support@shoppie.example** or **+91 80000 12345**, Monday–Saturday, **9:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. IST**, excluding public holidays. These are fictional sample contact details.

## 8. Changes to this policy

Shoppie may update this policy as its services change. The updated version and revision date will be posted here. The version available when an order is placed applies to that order, subject to applicable law.
