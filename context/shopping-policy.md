# Shoppie Shopping Policy

> **Synthetic sample policy:** This document is fictional starter content for product development and must be reviewed and adapted before being used with customers.

**Effective date:** 1 November 2026  
**Last updated:** 1 November 2026

## 1. About Shoppie

Shoppie is a fictional e-commerce business that sells and ships perishable and non-perishable goods to customers in India. This policy describes how customers can browse, order, pay for, and receive products through the Shoppie website and mobile app (together, the “Services”).

The `.example` email domain and phone number above are fictional sample data and are not operational contact details.

## 2. Products and availability

- Product descriptions, images, prices, pack sizes, and availability are shown on the product page. Images are illustrative; actual color, shape, or packaging may vary slightly.
- Products are subject to stock and service-area availability. Adding an item to a cart does not reserve it.
- Shoppie may limit quantities, correct listing errors, or cancel an order if an item is unavailable or a listing contains a material error. If Shoppie cancels a paid order, the customer will receive a refund to the original payment method.
- For perishable products, the listing may show a harvest, preparation, packing, or use-by date, as applicable. Customers should check the label on delivery and follow any storage instructions.
- Customers should review ingredients, allergen information, dimensions, and other product details before ordering. For questions about an ingredient or allergy, contact support before purchase.

## 3. Placing an order

1. Select products and quantities and add them to the cart.
2. Enter a complete delivery address and a reachable phone number.
3. Review the order summary, delivery fee, applicable taxes, and estimated delivery window.
4. Choose an available payment method and submit the order.

An order confirmation is sent by email or SMS after successful placement. The confirmation is an acknowledgement that Shoppie received the order; acceptance and fulfillment remain subject to availability, successful payment, and serviceability. Shoppie may contact the customer to confirm address or delivery details. If the customer cannot be reached and delivery cannot be completed, the order may be cancelled under the Cancellation Policy.

## 4. Pricing and payment

- Prices are displayed in Indian rupees (INR) and include applicable taxes unless the checkout page states otherwise.
- Delivery fees, handling fees, discounts, and any other charges are shown before the customer places the order.
- Available payment options are displayed at checkout. A payment is treated as successful when Shoppie receives confirmation from its payment provider.
- If a payment is debited but the order is not confirmed, contact support with the order or transaction reference. Shoppie will check the transaction and, if it did not receive the payment, the payment provider will generally reverse the debit according to its process.
- Promotional codes are subject to their displayed terms, expiry, product eligibility, and any minimum order value. Only one code may be used per order unless the offer says otherwise.

## 5. Delivery and shipping

- Shoppie currently uses **1–3 business days** as a sample delivery estimate for standard service areas and **same-day or next-day delivery** for eligible local orders. The estimate shown at checkout for the specific address takes precedence.
- The sample standard delivery fee is **₹49** for orders below **₹499** and free for orders of **₹499 or more**. Any applicable fee is shown at checkout before payment.
- Delivery estimates are not guaranteed and may change because of weather, traffic, carrier capacity, address issues, or other events outside Shoppie’s reasonable control. Shoppie will share material delays through the contact details on the order where practical.
- The customer is responsible for providing an accurate address, access instructions, and a reachable phone number. Additional delivery attempts or re-delivery fees, if any, will be communicated before they are charged.
- A delivery is complete when the order is handed to the customer, an authorized recipient, or left at a location the customer has expressly approved.

### Perishable products

- Perishable goods (for example, fresh produce, dairy, chilled items, and prepared foods) are packed using packaging selected for the product and route. Temperature-controlled delivery is used where specified on the product or checkout page.
- Customers should be available to receive perishable orders during the displayed delivery window and promptly refrigerate, freeze, or otherwise store items according to their labels.
- Shoppie does not recommend leaving perishable products unattended. If the customer requests a safe drop-off, the customer accepts the risk of deterioration after the carrier records delivery, subject to applicable consumer rights and the Returns Policy.
- If an item arrives damaged, spoiled, leaking, warm when it should be chilled, or otherwise unsafe, the customer should stop using it and report the issue promptly under the Returns Policy.

### Non-perishable products

- Non-perishable goods are shipped using standard protective packaging suitable for the product.
- Customers should inspect packages on receipt. If outer packaging is visibly damaged or an item is missing, note the issue with the delivery partner where possible and contact support within **48 hours**.

## 6. Order changes and cancellation

Customers may request an order change or cancellation before the order is packed for dispatch. Once dispatch has begun, changes may not be possible. Eligibility, refunds, and any product-specific limits are described in the [Cancellation Policy](cancellation-policy.md).

## 7. Returns and issue reporting

Return, replacement, and refund eligibility depends on the product type and issue. Perishable goods generally cannot be returned after delivery for hygiene and safety reasons, but Shoppie will review reports of damage, spoilage, incorrect items, or missing goods. Full details are in the [Returns Policy](returns-policy.md).

## 8. Customer account and security

Customers are responsible for keeping account credentials secure and for activity conducted through their account. Please contact support promptly if you suspect unauthorized access. Shoppie will use account and order information to process purchases, deliveries, support requests, and refunds in accordance with its separately published privacy notice.

## 9. Policy updates

Shoppie may update this policy as its services change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when an update is published. Changes will apply to orders placed after the updated policy is posted, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

## 10. Governing terms

This sample policy is intended for a fictional India-based service and uses IST and INR for illustration. Applicable consumer, food safety, privacy, tax, and other laws may grant rights that are not limited by this draft. The final version should be reviewed for the actual business, operating locations, product categories, and applicable law.
